Privacy policy

Your writing and mood check-ins are personal. This page explains what Lumivu stores, what stays under your control, and where other services are involved.

Prepared 17 October 2026 · EIDOVANT

No Lumivu account, advertisements or app analytics.

Journal entries, moods and practice history are stored locally. Lumivu does not upload them to the developer or provide cloud sync. The app does not encrypt local data; your device may include it in system backups.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Lumivu mobile application and its bundled privacy page. Lumivu is provided by EIDOVANT (the “publisher”, “we” or “us”). You can contact us at aizbimusic@gmail.com.

Lumivu offers self-care practices, optional mood check-ins, private writing and a companion scene. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Lumivu does not provide emergency support or monitor your entries.

What Lumivu stores and why

  • Your optional mood check-ins and journal entries: the mood you select, text you save, and associated dates and times. They let you revisit your own history. Your current mood may change the companion’s appearance and the practices suggested locally.
  • Your practice history: selected activities, start and completion times, progress, meditation playback position and listening progress, and completion identifiers. These let you resume a practice and keep the room’s earned growth.
  • Your preferences: music, ambience and reduced-motion settings, the next local quote and music track, a bounded visit count, and the day a mood invitation was last offered. These keep the experience consistent and limit repeated invitations.
  • Basic device settings used locally: language preference, local date and time, reduced-motion preference and whether the app is in the foreground. Lumivu uses these to format dates, respect motion settings and pause audio when appropriate.

Entries use randomly generated local record identifiers, not an advertising or account identifier. The app does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or payment-card details. You may choose to include personal or health information in your writing; Lumivu treats it as local text and does not send it to an AI service or a clinician.

Unsaved writing stays in memory. It may be lost if you close, reload or leave the app before saving.

Local storage, security and backups

Lumivu stores saved entries and practice history in the app’s local browser/WebView database (IndexedDB). Small display preferences use localStorage. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this journal data, and we cannot read or recover it remotely.

Lumivu does not add encryption to this local storage. Access depends on your device’s operating-system protections, screen lock, other software and security settings. Someone with access to your unlocked or compromised device may be able to access your entries.

Operating-system backups and device-transfer services may include app data. Android backup is enabled in the current app configuration; behavior on Android and iOS also depends on the device and your settings. These services are managed by the platform or device provider, not by Lumivu’s own cloud-sync service. Review your device’s backup controls if you do not want app data included.

Audio, transcripts and practices are included with the installed app. Using them does not require a streaming account or uploading your entries. Downloads, updates, store licensing and external links may still require an internet connection.

Retention and deleting your data

Saved local data remains until you delete it, clear app/browser storage, or the operating system removes it. Lumivu does not automatically expire your journal entries.

  1. Delete one item: open a saved journal entry or mood check-in and choose Delete entry. Deleting an entry does not remove the associated practice-completion history or earned room growth.
  2. Delete local app data: open Settings, choose Delete all data and confirm. After the operation saves successfully, this resets notes, check-ins, sessions, growth and settings, clears unsaved drafts, and removes the app’s local rotation and invitation preferences.
  3. Manage other copies separately: use your device or backup provider’s controls for existing system backups. Lumivu’s deletion function cannot remove those backups, screenshots or other copies you made.

If a save or deletion fails, the app shows a retry state; do not assume the change has been stored until it succeeds. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove local entries, but a system restore may bring back data from an older backup.

There is no Lumivu account to delete and no in-app export feature. We cannot delete or retrieve a device’s local journal through a support email. Store purchase records and any correspondence are handled separately below.

Other services and sharing

App data: Lumivu does not sell journal data, display advertisements, run an analytics or crash-reporting SDK, or share saved moods and entries with the publisher or another service. The app has no public profiles, social feed or messaging between users.

External links: source and licence credits may open third-party websites when you choose a link. Those providers can receive normal web-request information, such as your IP address and browser details, and apply their own privacy policies. Lumivu does not attach your journal entries or mood history to those links.

Google Play: if you buy or download Lumivu through Google Play, Google handles your store account, payment and its platform services under its own terms and privacy policy. Lumivu does not receive your card details and contains no in-app billing or subscriptions. The publisher may access order, refund and related information that Google makes available in Play Console, or that you send for support. This information is separate from your local journal.

Google Play may also perform licensing, security or platform diagnostics under its own terms. These are separate from Lumivu’s local journal functionality.

Support messages: if you email aizbimusic@gmail.com, EIDOVANT and Google’s Gmail service receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending journal entries or sensitive health details when they are not needed to explain a technical issue. We use correspondence to respond to requests and handle relevant purchase or refund questions. Access is for the people handling those requests and relevant account administration.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain a record of its resolution. Purchase or dispute records may be kept for applicable accounting, legal or dispute-handling requirements. You can ask us to delete correspondence we no longer need to retain. Google may process email and store information in countries other than yours under its privacy terms.

Viewing this policy online

The bundled page works offline. This HTML document contains no scripts, analytics, forms, advertising or remote fonts. The public version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the connection information needed to serve the page and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. EIDOVANT does not add analytics or tracking to this page. Viewing the policy does not send your local journal or mood history to GitHub.

Intended audience

Lumivu is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. Teenagers should seek guidance from a parent, caregiver or healthcare professional when they need help. The app does not ask for age or date of birth and does not enable contact between users.

Private text is not monitored, and the companion is not a person or a professional support service. A parent or guardian can contact us about personal information a child may have included in a support message and request its deletion, subject to any required record retention. Local entries can be removed using the device controls described above.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when the app’s data practices change. The bundled copy reflects the app version you installed. Any new collection or sharing must be explained before it is introduced where required.

Questions and privacy requests

Contact the publisher about this policy, support correspondence or purchase records. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information the publisher holds. Local journal data stays under your device controls; we cannot access it to fulfill a remote request.
